# Data provenance and fixture contract

All datasets in this release were constructed for this book project or generated by the included code. None represent a real company, customer or person. No external data download is required. There are no credentials, private records or third-party datasets in the archive.

- V1C01: fixed two-dimensional vectors, 2×2 linear system, 4×2 PCA matrix. Numerical fixtures agreed with Chapter 1.
- V1C02: fixed scalar function and one-hidden-unit network. No training dataset; a single illustrative pair x=2, target=4.
- V1C03: constructed defective-parcel population of 100,000, fixed discrete demand distribution, and a separate seeded Monte Carlo simulation (303). The constructed counts are exact teaching counts, not the random simulation's counts.
- V1C04: five constructed values [48,52,49,51,50]; Bernoulli count 7/10; independent exponential draws (404) for LLN and CLT examples.
- V1C05: two constructed points x=[1,2], y=[2,4]; quadratic Hessian diag(1,100).
- V1C06: fixed probability vectors and logits. No sampled data.
- V1C07: exact finite prediction/noise ensembles; separate train/validation/test draws (707); repeated independent training draws (708). Different sequential RNG draws form separate independent samples under the generator. All model fitting and feature scaling use training rows only.
- V1C08: eight constructed labels/probabilities and a three-row regression example; random synthetic entity data (808); chronological feature-availability counterexample (809).

The human-readable CSV files mirror small fixed inputs. The `lab.py` literals and generators are the executable fixture sources. If changing an input for a new edition, reconcile CSVs, code, expected results, tests and both manuscripts.

The author has not yet selected a new code reuse licence. This author-review package makes no new licence grant. Public code availability is the latest approved access choice; see the expanded-release note below. Dependencies are installed separately under their upstream licences; see DEPENDENCIES.md.

## Expanded release 1.1.0

- V1C09: exact four-paradigm fixtures; KMeans seed 909. The masked-token probability is supplied; no language model or external corpus. RL values are simulated arithmetic, not a trained deployment policy.
- V1C10: constructed four-row regression training / two-row test data, supplied logistic coefficients; synthetic duration generator seed 1010 with 400 rows; synthetic replenishment generator seed 1011 with 800 rows. Independent split indices are saved.
- V1C11: constructed Bernoulli marginal counts, three-point distance geometry and four-point SVM; synthetic replenishment seed 1111 with 1,000 rows. 500 training / 250 calibration / 250 test rows, with all scalers and base models fitted on training only. Three genuinely fitted calibrators see calibration rows only.
- V1C12: constructed 12-row impurity fixture and four-row boosting fixture; synthetic replenishment seed 1212 with 800 rows. Family selection uses 480 train / 160 validation; final refits use those 640 development rows and evaluate on 160 held-out test rows. No within-family hyperparameter search.
- V1C13: constructed six-point clustering and separate four-point PCA fixtures; three activity-cloud generator seed 1313, 360 rows, split 270/90. Scaling, PCA and k-means fit only training rows.

The replenishment generator uses independent fictional product-period rows with stock-cover days, demand index, promotion flag and lead time. Label probability follows a disclosed logistic formula with a promotion/low-stock interaction in `classical_common.py`. No actual store stock, prices, food labels, customer demographics, private transactions or courier information is read. Random IID rows are a deliberate simplification, not a production time-series validation design.

The author's latest choice is public code access for everyone, without a purchase gate. No manuscripts, supplied collections, investor documents or confidential project records are in this package. Public availability does not itself grant a new reuse licence. Dependency binaries are not bundled, and no new licence has been chosen on the author's behalf.

## Additive ShopSmart cases

- V1C05-CASE02: 3×3 fixed whole-pack product-price matrix in Australian cents; three single-store fees; hypothetical per-store pickup and consolidated-courier fees. Exhaustive enumeration of 27 assignments. All prices, stock/eligibility flags, equivalence and service permissions are hypothetical, not retailer data.
- V1C10-CASE02: fixed past days −6,−3,0, synthetic washing-powder prices8,9,10 and chocolate prices6,6,6 AUD; OLS ordinary-state forecast for day3. Promotion prices/probability and delay costs are separate assumed scenarios, not trained predictions or real sales. Cadbury is contextual naming only, without an asserted actual SKU or pack size. This generic basket is unrelated to the proposed vegan/vegetarian dinner example.

These cases add only local arithmetic and tests. Typed, voice and upload adapters, real-time data integrations, product safety assessment and fulfillment are not implemented.
